MY CONTRIBUTION

Defenses, waves
and player control.

I programmed defense systems, camera controls and the enemy wave director, and contributed to game balance and design.

The project was developed as a team in Unity and C#, resulting in a completed, playable 3D tower defense game. My work connected the systems that control the player’s view, the defenses they use and the flow of enemies they face.

01

Defense systems

Shared defense stats and attack cooldowns, with separate behavior classes for target selection and firing.

02

Enemy wave director

Wave spawning scales the enemy count by level. The level manager controls target-angle ranges and advances rounds when enemies are defeated.

03

Camera & balance

Orbit controls, adjustable camera height and scroll-wheel zoom keep the map in view. I also contributed to gameplay tuning.
